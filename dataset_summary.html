Datasets#

170 movement datasets are catalogued across twelve human modality lanes and one animal lane: 159 human, 11 animal (counts read from the registry at build time). The cell codes in the tables are defined on the Taxonomy page. Every dataset keeps ONE identity (one name, one card, one download) however many modality streams it carries; multimodal sets appear in each family table they contribute to. Animal entries (mouse, rat and macaque models of movement disorders) carry species and model metadata and map to symptom analogs in free text; they never mix into the human clinical taxonomy cells.

Access cases#

  • hosted: served from MoveAtlas’s own verified records; one call downloads. (Today: the local record store; MoveAtlas’s public Zenodo / Hugging Face records open with the public launch.)

  • recipe: fetched from the original host by the documented recipe.

  • local_only: private-tier data; nothing downloads, a local path is required. Empty since 2026-09-01: the CODY derived release is public on Zenodo (CC BY 4.0, 10.5281/zenodo.22232609) and the CODY entries sit in the hosted tier; raw patient video stays in-house, outside the atlas.

  • barrier: a documented access barrier; the package raises the documented route instead of a download.
