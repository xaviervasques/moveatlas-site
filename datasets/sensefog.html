Every session parsed clean (no unrecognized channel, no read failure); two sessions carry p99 > 1000 uV and are flagged “review” in processed/eeg_ingest_audit.csv. validate_canonical –family eeg,emg: 0 errors (sampled), and the –full pass of 2026-09-07 (every eeg + emg record of the corpus opened) raised ZERO warnings on sensefog - the 262 corpus warnings all sit on ds007526 / ds002778 / ds004584 amplitude bands (case 3 territory). BrainVision markers ride verbatim as events; NO FOG label array was emitted (the .vmrk holds Moblab/Toggle sync markers only - a per-frame FOG label would be a guess). DEFERRED to their own verified readers: STN Percept LFP (ieeg/.json, Medtronic export -> intracranial family), APDM Opal motion (motion/.h5, ankles + L5 -> imu family), FOG annotation source (likely the motion .mat side), and the three derived-analysis folders (Coherence-Data, Kinematic-Data, Time-Frequency-Data, Table & Figures) which are not raw signal.

PASS-2 PREP, Percept LFP inventory (read 2026-09-07, 44 JSON): 43/44 sessions carry BrainSenseTimeDomain: 86 packets, ALL 250 Hz, bipolar STN pairs per hemisphere (ONE_THREE_LEFT 32 / _RIGHT 30, ZERO_TWO_L 9 / _R 5, ZERO_THREE_L 2 / _R 8), packet durations 14 s to 407 s, 18,013 s total (~5.0 h of LFP). Stimulation status initial/final: OFF/OFF in 41 sessions, OFF->ON 2, ON->OFF 1 - the recordings are essentially stim-OFF sensing. Each packet ships TicksInMses (250 ms ticks), GlobalSequences + GlobalPacketSizes (62/63 samples per tick) and a Gain: the reader MUST rebuild the time axis from the sequence counters and MASK the missing ticks (Percept packet loss is the known pitfall; the corpus’s validity mask is the right home for it), and must convert TimeDomainData via the documented Gain rather than assume uV. Also present: BrainSenseLfp (2 Hz power stream), LeadConfiguration, Impedance, Groups (program settings), DiagnosticData. PatientEvents (8 files) are clinical state markers in German (“Off Anfang”, “On Anfang”, “Dyskinesien Anfang”, “Off” = medication OFF/ON/dyskinesia onsets), NOT FOG annotations.
