Taxonomy#

Every dataset in the registry carries one moveatlas_cells string, for example B2, D3; M2: the clinical or context cells it covers, then the modality lanes it records. This page defines the codes. The table below is generated at build time: the cell codes come from build_taxonomy_map.py (the validator every mapping passes through), the names from build_portal.py (the portal’s canonical mapping), and the HPO column from docs/hpo_movement_terms.csv, the subtree of HP:0100022 “Abnormality of movement” projected onto the cells by build_hpo_movement_map.py. If the code and name tables ever disagree, the site build fails instead of picking one.

Five classes, one modality axis#

  • A, hyperkinetic (A1-A8): tremor, myoclonus, tics, dystonia, stereotypies, athetosis, chorea, ballismus. The atlas’s centre of gravity: this is where open data is scarcest.

  • B, hypokinetic (B1-B5): rigidity, bradykinesia, hypokinesia, akinesia, freezing of gait.

  • C, age-conditioned normal (C1-C3): the healthy lifespan axis (adult, infant, aging) that every pathological cell is read against.

  • D, neurological comorbidity (D1-D7) and E, systemic comorbidity (E1-E7): context lanes, never targets on their own.

  • M, modality codes (M1-M12): the recording lane, from pose and inertial signals to clinical scales.

A dataset counts in every cell and lane it covers. Animal entries (mouse, rat and macaque models) map to symptom analogs in free text on their own cards and never enter the human cells, so each coverage matrix stays single-species. Empty cells are findings, not omissions: the ledger further down records the dated searches behind each one.

Confirmed absences ledger#

The negative results of the dated data hunts, included verbatim from docs/hunt_confirmed_absences.md (the file the resource paper cites). “Absent” means no public dataset was found by systematic search of the named repositories and the literature at the stated date.

Negative results of the two dated hunts (P1 morning + second hunt afternoon, 2026-08-22; four parallel web sweeps: imaging/genetics, electrophysiology, video/pose/pediatric/aging, comorbidities/histology/scales). “Absent” means: no public dataset found by systematic search of Zenodo, Figshare, PhysioNet, OpenNeuro, IEEE DataPort, OSF, Dryad, institutional repositories and the 2023-2026 literature, at the stated date. These absences justify the internal production protocols and are themselves a publishable finding of the resource paper.

Signal-level absences (phenomenology cells)#

  • A2 Myoclonus EMG at capture grade (>= 1000 Hz surface EMG): NOTHING public. Polymyography stays in clinical archives; only case-report figures exist. Consequence: internal high-rate protocol is the only route.

  • A3 Tics video: REVISION PENDING VERIFICATION (2026-09-04 closing sweep). Two candidates surfaced on generalist repositories: MindTS-MMD (Mendeley 10.17632/kswgcms346.1: 60 children with Tourette, >120 h of video+audio+text sessions, expert tic-frequency annotations, CC BY 4.0 claimed, “de-identified” claimed) and OSF 46vxm (video-derived tic detection data). NEITHER is verified: for both, the de-identification claim and actual signal content must be checked BEFORE the paper claims refutation (video of minors - the SSBD ethics precedent). Until then the paper wording is: “one annotated pediatric corpus appeared in 2026 and is catalogued pending verification”. The pre-sweep statement (all 2021-2026 tic-detection papers use private clinical video) still described the field accurately at its date.

  • A5 Stereotypies (clinical): REVISED 2026-09-04 (closing sweep). The absence is REFUTED at the skeleton level: ASDPose (Dinstein lab, JAMA Netw Open 2024; github.com/Dinstein-Lab/ASDMotion) distributes 7,352 manually annotated SMM segments from 241 children as OpenPose skeleton time-series, freely downloadable (licence verification pending; see datasets/pose/asdpose/datacard.md). The claim SURVIVES for raw video: still nothing public beyond ethics-gated sets (SSBD/SSBD+, AV-ASD: identifiable minors) and MMASD derivatives (licence unspecified). Paper wording: “no open stereotypy VIDEO corpus; an annotated skeleton corpus appeared in 2024 and is catalogued”.

  • A6 Athetosis / A8 Ballismus: zero public movement recordings anywhere; only the (private) CODY cohorts and feature tables from the cody-pipeline release.

  • A7 Chorea video/pose: no public HD video corpus (TRACK-HD/IMAGE-HD never released; 2026 chorea-wearable study keeps data private). Wrist accelerometry (hd_accel_jnet) and gait force (gaitndd) remain the only open signals.

  • B1 Rigidity, instrumented: no public dataset (MJFF refused); T8 internal protocol stands.

  • D7 Functional movement disorders: NO public video or sensor dataset at all (2025 MDS review of video-based models lists none). Only proxy: TIM-TREMOR’s functional-tremor arm (labels unverified; G0 re-admission decision pending).

Comorbidity / systemic absences#

  • RBD polysomnography beyond CAP: zero open PSG with RSWA/atonia scoring (Mayo, Montreal, Innsbruck cohorts private). D6 stays CAP-only until the Beijing sleep-LFP application (121 patients, PSG+LFP, not public) succeeds.

  • Wilson disease (E2), thyroid tremor (E1), tardive dyskinesia (E6), catatonia (E7): zero public movement datasets; only copyrighted teaching clips in journals.

  • Diabetic neuropathy gait (E3/D4): nothing open today; Sacco-lab data never deposited; DFN-DS (135 diabetic subjects, in-shoe pressure) announced as “will be available”: WATCH LIST. Chemo-induced neuropathy arm of clinical_gait_signals_2025 is the current best neuropathic-gait source (ND license flag).

Imaging / genetics absences#

  • Open DAT-SPECT: none outside PPMI (application-gated). No open neuromelanin MRI PD set on OpenNeuro or Sci Data.

  • Open Huntington MRI: none (Enroll-HD imaging application-only). Essential tremor and dystonia MRI on OpenNeuro: essentially absent beyond small DBS case series.

  • Genotype-phenotype bulk: MDSGene has no export and a no-redistribution license; the only redistributable substrate is HPO + ClinVar + PanelApp (all queued).

Standing consequences#

  1. Internal production protocols (T8 rigidity, high-rate myoclonus, GMA neonatal cohort, DBS archive) have NO public substitute: they stay on the critical path (plan v2, phase 0/1).

  2. CODY is confirmed as a globally unique asset for A2/A3/A5/A6/A8.

  3. The resource paper can claim these absences with dated, documented searches.

Phenotype-level absences (2026-09-05 deep-dive sweep)#

Finer-grained than the A-cell absences above; each verified by a dedicated sub-agent against Zenodo/figshare/Mendeley/OSF/PhysioNet/ OpenNeuro/IEEE DataPort + the papers’ own availability statements:

  • BLEPHAROSPASM: no openly downloadable patient EMG/video/blink dataset anywhere; the npj Digit Med 2025 paper (847+179 videos, closed) itself states “No publicly available blepharospasm dataset currently exists”. Nearest open item: 5 healthy-control orbicularis EMG sessions (Mendeley nm535v73jj).

  • WRITER’S CRAMP / focal hand dystonia: no open handwriting- kinematics, sEMG, or mocap deposit; the Duke MovAlyzer set (21 WC) is corresponding-author-request only.

  • OCULOMOTOR (all movement disorders): zero open eye-tracking recordings - PD antisaccade, PSP vertical gaze, HD saccades all live in papers or gated cohorts only.

  • RAW FACIAL VIDEO (hypomimia, tardive orofacial): structurally absent (identifiable faces); the Harvard OpenFace-features CSV set is the only open derivative.

  • UNGATED levodopa-induced-dyskinesia sensor data: none exists; cis_pd/real_pd (Synapse-gated) are the only dyskinesia-labeled sensor corpora found. Counterpoint the same sweep produced: cervical dystonia now HAS one open multimodal signal deposit (mrc_lfp_dystonia, CC BY-SA, login tier) - the A1 desert has one oasis.

  • WILSON’S DISEASE: no downloadable movement/speech signal anywhere (tremor accelerometry, gait, handwriting, oculography all searched); the Anhui 52-subject wearable-tremor and 110-subject speech corpora exist in papers only. The E2 absence above is re-confirmed at phenotype depth.

  • PSP/MSA/CBD: one open sensor deposit now exists (mobilityapp_atypical, 20 PSP + 23 MSA - catalogued 2026-09-05); CBD specifically remains at zero signal datasets; atypical-parkinsonism OCULOMOTOR remains zero.

  • PREMANIFEST HD KINEMATICS: nothing open beyond the wrist accelerometry we hold (hd_accel_jnet); TRACK-HD/PREDICT-HD/Enroll-HD raw Q-Motor and eye traces are application-tier (enroll_hd_family).

  • RLS ACTIGRAPHY / patient SIT: no open dataset; PLM-scored PSG exists open only in cap_sleep (10 patients), at scale only behind the NSRR DAUA (nsrr_plm_psg).

  • ORTHOSTATIC TREMOR: zero deposited leg-sEMG/accelerometry (the 13-18 Hz signature lives only in papers and one case-report video); PALATAL TREMOR and HEMIFACIAL SPASM likewise at zero deposits. Counterpoint: SPASMODIC DYSPHONIA is now the best-served focal dystonia - the Saarbruecken Voice Database SD subset (voice+EGG, CC BY, catalogued svd_spasmodic_dysphonia) is fully open.

  • BROKEN AVAILABILITY STATEMENT sighting (case-11 family): the LSR hemifacial-spasm CNN paper (PLOS ONE 2022) declares “all data on GitHub”; the repo holds two notebooks and no EMG.

  • TARDIVE DYSKINESIA and AKATHISIA (E6 depth): both at zero deposited signal; every AIMS-scored video corpus (TDtect, iRxReminder) is proprietary, the classic akathisia actometry predates data sharing; only scale-score IPD exists behind Vivli/NIMH-NDA DUAs. Open adjacent context: OBF-Psychiatric and PSYKOSE actigraphy (antipsychotic-exposed, unlabeled).

  • D7 FUNCTIONAL revised 2026-09-05: two genuine open deposits exist (osf_fmd_eeg processed EEG; osf_fmd_kinematics limb velocity traces, both licence-pending) - and TIM-TREMOR, the old functional- tremor proxy, is now CONFIRMED WITHDRAWN at 4TU (HTTP 410): a catalogued dataset died at the source between our July attempt and today.

  • PEDIATRIC/GENETIC RARE (2026-09-05 paediatric pass): SYDENHAM CHOREA is the cleanest absolute gap found (zero datasets, zero deposited recordings, zero registries with video). Also at zero open signal: SGCE myoclonus-dystonia, PRRT2 attack recordings, NBIA/PKAN, Rett hand stereotypies, Angelman gait, ataxia-telangiectasia, DYT1 generalized dystonia. A NEW ACCESS-MODEL CLASS surfaced: the FAMILY-MEDIATED REGISTRY (GNAO1.org, ADCY5.org, A-T RARE-X) where patients/families control researcher access. CODY is independently confirmed (arXiv 2602.00163) as covering ADCY5- and SGCE-type phenotypes - the corpus’s own deposit services two of these gaps.

  • CROSS-REPOSITORY VERDICT (2026-09-05 cross-repository pass, seven repositories swept systematically): Huntington/chorea SIGNAL data is absent from ALL of EBRAINS/DABI/OpenNeuro/PhysioNet/Dryad/GIN/ Synapse; myoclonus absent everywhere; Tourette signal exists in EXACTLY ONE place on earth (DABI 1UH3NS119844, gated); invasive movement-disorder ephys lives in DABI and Dryad, NOT in OpenNeuro/ EBRAINS/GIN.

Access-barriers casebook#

How often “open” data is not practically open, documented case by case with the evidence trail the paper cites. The casebook itself stays in the repository; its case titles are listed here from the file at build time.

13 documented cases (docs/ACCESS_BARRIERS_CASES.md, each with its evidence trail):

  • Case 1: published but never deposited (beijing_sleep_lfp)

  • Case 2: data in hand, licence self-contradictory (care_pd)

  • Case 3: data public, values contradict their declaration (ds007526)

  • Case 4: open licence, closed format (gait120)

  • Case 5: the transport arms race (figshare, resolved by changing CLIENT)

  • Case 6: the open-data deserts, quantified (2026-09-01 sweep)

  • Case 7: published, benchmarked by a whole field, and licence-free

  • Case 8: “open data” that turns out to be figures (lance_adams)

  • Case 9: no human patient neuron morphology exists (neuromorpho_bg)

  • Case 10: the animal kingdom repeats the human asymmetry (2026-09-01)

  • Case 11: “the data are with this article”, and they are not (2026-09-02)

  • Case 12: our own deposit, audited like a stranger’s (2026-09-03/04)

  • Case 13: withdrawn after publication, badges still green (piet_essential_tremor, 2026-09-07)
