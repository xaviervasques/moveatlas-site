
Negative results of the two dated hunts (P1 morning + second hunt afternoon,

video/pose/pediatric/aging, comorbidities/histology/scales). "Absent" means: no

## Signal-level absences (phenomenology cells)

- A2 Myoclonus EMG at capture grade (>= 1000 Hz surface EMG): NOTHING public.
  Polymyography stays in clinical archives; only case-report figures exist.
  Consequence: internal high-rate protocol is the only route.
- A3 Tics video: REVISION PENDING VERIFICATION (2026-09-04 closing
  sweep). Two candidates surfaced on generalist repositories: MindTS-MMD
  (Mendeley 10.17632/kswgcms346.1: 60 children with Tourette, >120 h of
  video+audio+text sessions, expert tic-frequency annotations, CC BY 4.0
  claimed, "de-identified" claimed) and OSF 46vxm (video-derived tic
  detection data). NEITHER is verified: for both, the de-identification
  claim and actual signal content must be checked BEFORE the paper
  claims refutation (video of minors - the SSBD ethics precedent).
  Until then the paper wording is: "one annotated pediatric corpus
  appeared in 2026 and is catalogued pending verification". The
  pre-sweep statement (all 2021-2026 tic-detection papers use private
  clinical video) still described the field accurately at its date.
- A5 Stereotypies (clinical): REVISED 2026-09-04 (closing sweep). The
  absence is REFUTED at the skeleton level: ASDPose (Dinstein lab, JAMA
  Netw Open 2024; github.com/Dinstein-Lab/ASDMotion) distributes 7,352
  manually annotated SMM segments from 241 children as OpenPose skeleton
  time-series, freely downloadable (licence verification pending; see
  datasets/pose/asdpose/datacard.md). The claim SURVIVES for raw video:
  still nothing public beyond ethics-gated sets (SSBD/SSBD+, AV-ASD:
  identifiable minors) and MMASD derivatives (licence unspecified).
  Paper wording: "no open stereotypy VIDEO corpus; an annotated skeleton
  corpus appeared in 2024 and is catalogued".
- A6 Athetosis / A8 Ballismus: zero public movement recordings anywhere; only
  the (private) CODY cohorts and feature tables from the cody-pipeline release.
- A7 Chorea video/pose: no public HD video corpus (TRACK-HD/IMAGE-HD never
  released; 2026 chorea-wearable study keeps data private). Wrist accelerometry
  (hd_accel_jnet) and gait force (gaitndd) remain the only open signals.
- B1 Rigidity, instrumented: no public dataset (MJFF refused); T8 internal
  protocol stands.
- D7 Functional movement disorders: NO public video or sensor dataset at all
  (2025 MDS review of video-based models lists none). Only proxy: TIM-TREMOR's
  functional-tremor arm (labels unverified; G0 re-admission decision pending).

## Phenotype-level absences (2026-09-05 deep-dive sweep)

Finer-grained than the A-cell absences above; each verified by a
dedicated sub-agent against Zenodo/figshare/Mendeley/OSF/PhysioNet/
OpenNeuro/IEEE DataPort + the papers' own availability statements:
- BLEPHAROSPASM: no openly downloadable patient EMG/video/blink
  dataset anywhere; the npj Digit Med 2025 paper (847+179 videos,
  closed) itself states "No publicly available blepharospasm dataset
  currently exists". Nearest open item: 5 healthy-control orbicularis
  EMG sessions (Mendeley nm535v73jj).
- WRITER'S CRAMP / focal hand dystonia: no open handwriting-
  kinematics, sEMG, or mocap deposit; the Duke MovAlyzer set (21 WC)
  is corresponding-author-request only.
- OCULOMOTOR (all movement disorders): zero open eye-tracking
  recordings - PD antisaccade, PSP vertical gaze, HD saccades all
  live in papers or gated cohorts only.
- RAW FACIAL VIDEO (hypomimia, tardive orofacial): structurally
  absent (identifiable faces); the Harvard OpenFace-features CSV set
  is the only open derivative.
- UNGATED levodopa-induced-dyskinesia sensor data: none exists;
  cis_pd/real_pd (Synapse-gated) are the only dyskinesia-labeled
  sensor corpora found.
Counterpoint the same sweep produced: cervical dystonia now HAS one
open multimodal signal deposit (mrc_lfp_dystonia, CC BY-SA, login
tier) - the A1 desert has one oasis.
- WILSON'S DISEASE: no downloadable movement/speech signal anywhere

  the Anhui 52-subject wearable-tremor and 110-subject speech corpora

  phenotype depth.
- PSP/MSA/CBD: one open sensor deposit now exists (mobilityapp_atypical,
  20 PSP + 23 MSA - catalogued 2026-09-05); CBD specifically remains at

- PREMANIFEST HD KINEMATICS: nothing open beyond the wrist accelerometry
